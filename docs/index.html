Paksward

A password manager that keeps everything on your computer.

Download for your computer

Your computer will show a warning. That is expected.

Paksward is not code-signed (signing costs money), so the system does not recognise the author.

  • Windows: unzip, open Paksward.exe. If SmartScreen appears: More info, then Run anyway.
  • Mac: unzip and open Paksward. It will be blocked. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Paksward, click Open Anyway, and confirm. Only needed the first time. (On older macOS, right-click the app and choose Open works too.)
  • Linux: unzip and run ./Paksward from a terminal.

To check your download is genuine, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt.

What it does with your data

Paksward runs only on your computer. It has no accounts, no cloud and no server. The one optional exception is a leaked-password check that you start yourself: it sends only 5-character fingerprints, never your passwords. On first start it makes two random keys (Secret Key and Recovery Key) and shows them once. Save them. If you lose your password and your keys, nobody can open your vault.

It does not run on phones yet.
